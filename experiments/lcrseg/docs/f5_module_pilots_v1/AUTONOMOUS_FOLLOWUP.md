## Operational success criterion

Use the existing paired practical gate, applied to both the matched F5 parent and the strongest available B2 reference: average orders within each seed, both seed Final increments positive, mean Final increment at least0.005, each order's mean Old and Incoming delta at least−0.005, and each individual seed/order/domain/rim-or-cup delta at least−0.05. Always report B0 and all negative outcomes as well. A small F5-only gain or one favorable seed is not overall success.

Retrospective B2 aggregates remain explicitly historical comparisons. Reuse frozen comparable controls or introduce a necessary matched control in a new protocol if implementation/protocol changes make the historical comparison insufficient. Do not change the criterion after looking at results.

Passing this criterion establishes development progress, not independent patient generalization, SOTA, clinical efficacy, or novelty. Repeatedly reused development data remain disclosed. No guarantee of eventual success is made.
