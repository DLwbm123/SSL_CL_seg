## Why this version exists

and contains `prototypes=None`. Original v2 explicitly required that missing
tensor. Its partial 265 validation caches are retained and not reused.

The separately preregistered recovery ran both GPUs through the exact original

historical bank hash. That limitation is carried into every v2.1 report.
